# Release 003: the iPhone app on the App Store

**Why and when:** Joe wants Story listed publicly, so that someone can find it without a TestFlight invitation. Later: after [Release 001](001-publish-the-first-release.md) has friends using the TestFlight build, and when Joe is ready to write the policy pages. This is a sketch of what review requires; plan the steps in detail then.

## Status

- **Effort**: L
- **Risk**: MEDIUM. Review outcomes are not predictable.
- **Depends on**: Release 001; [Sharing 001](../sharing/001-join-on-iphone-first.md) if the listing is for people who start on a phone
- **Category**: release

## What review requires that Story lacks

- **Account deletion in the app.** A person who joins a host has an account. The app offers only "Disconnect and Pair Again", which leaves the account and its trees on the host. Deleting needs a host route and a decision on what happens to trees the person shared.
- **Report and block for shared content.** People, profiles, photos and shared trees are content from other people. Review expects a way to report it, a way to block a person, terms the person accepts, and a contact who acts on reports.
- **Policy pages.** A privacy policy URL, a support URL, and the App Privacy answers.
- **Listing.** Screenshots for iPhone and iPad, description, age rating, and a review note that explains the Siri voice-note phrases.

## What it has already

- **A way in for the reviewer.** Look Around First (TestFlight build 2) shows sample pages on a phone with no account. Whether App Store review accepts sample pages alone is not known; beta review of the same build will say something. If it does not, [Sharing 001](../sharing/001-join-on-iphone-first.md) lets a reviewer join from an invitation link on the phone, which replaces Joe's 2026-10-10 decision not to build joining on the iPhone.

## Open questions

- Is the public listing for people with their own host, for people invited to someone's host, or both? The first-launch screen follows from the answer, and so does whether this waits for Sharing 001: until then a person who finds the app with no Mac can only look around.
- Does account deletion remove the person's trees from the host, or only the account?
